# Age and consent terms of the services Momentum uses

MOM-116. Read on 2026-10-26. This is a record of what each service's own published terms say about minimum age and consent, so the product owner can decide what to do. **It is not legal advice.**

How to read it:

- Every quote is the service's own text, copied from its page on the date read. A term that could not be found is marked "not found", with where it was looked for. Nothing is assumed.
- The pages were fetched and searched by a tool, not read line by line by a person. Check any quote you will rely on against the live page; the links are in the table.
- "Binds" says whose agreement the term is: the developer or account holder who signs up for the service, or the person who connects their own account to Momentum.
- What Momentum sends is described by category only.

## Open questions

1. **Anthropic Usage Policy date.** The page shows "Nov 12, 2026", which is after the date it was read. It may be an announced future version or a page error. Re-read it on or after that date and confirm the minors wording is unchanged.
2. **Hevy dates.** The terms page shows an effective date of October 30, 2026 and also "takes effect on October 1, 2026". Confirm which is current.
3. **Email sender.** Confirm who actually delivers Supabase's built-in auth email and whether that sender has terms of its own. If a custom mail provider is added later, its terms need a row here.
4. **Supabase terms.** No age term was found in the terms, only the under-13 rule in the privacy policy. Ask Supabase, or read the full page by eye, before relying on "none".
5. **Cloudflare Turnstile.** Read Turnstile's own terms and Cloudflare's privacy policy if it is the chosen provider.
6. **Quote check.** The pages were read through an automated fetch. Confirm the quoted sentences on the live pages before they are relied on or cited elsewhere.
